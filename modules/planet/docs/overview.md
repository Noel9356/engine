# planet module

planet generates procedural 3D planets: a signed density field shaped by continents, ridged mountains, hills, terraces and true 3D displacement (overhangs, rock strata, optional caves), classified into climates and biomes, meshed with marching cubes into LOD chunk meshes, and streamed through an octree that keeps the working set hugging the terrain shell. Every chunk mesh is a pure function of the planet parameters and the chunk's integer coordinates, which makes generation deterministic and independent of chunk build order.

## Capability

The module provides the `planet` capability (ABI version constant `planetCapabilityAbi = 1`, see `include/engine/planet/capabilities.hpp`). Planet handles are `std::uint32_t`, never reused, with `0` (`invalidPlanet`) meaning "none". `PlanetDescription` layers per-planet overrides (seed, radius, sea level offset, center) over the module's `[planet]` configuration; a validated `PlanetGenerationParams` snapshot is available through `copyGenerationParams`.

Entries: `createPlanet`, `destroyPlanet`, `updatePlanet` (per-frame LOD advance from an eye position), `visibleChunkCapacity` + `copyVisibleChunks` (borrowed views of the ready chunk meshes, valid until the next `updatePlanet`), `sampleSurfaceRadius` (first density crossing along a unit direction), `sampleClimate` (biome/temperature/moisture at a surface point), `sampleRelief` (radial height plus the mountain/cliff masks - consumers can locate specific landforms such as terraced cliff country), `copyStatistics`, and `hashChunk` (synchronous pure build + FNV-1a hash, the determinism self-test hook).

## Provided / required / optional capabilities

- `module.toml`: `provides = ["planet"]`, `requires = []`.
- Entry table (bottom of `source/planet_module.cpp`): one provided capability, no required or optional capabilities, no declared permissions.

## Architecture

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `module.toml` | Module descriptor: name, version, provided capability, declared `config_keys` (every `[planet]` key). |
| `config.toml` | Defaults for the `[planet]` configuration section (see `configuration.md`). |
| `include/engine/planet/types.hpp` | Vocabulary: double-precision `Vec3` (planet-space math needs doubles at arbitrary radii), SplitMix64/FNV-1a/hashing, chunk keys (`makeChunkKey` packs depth + 20-bit lattice coordinates), `Biome` enum, `ClimateSample`, `PlanetStatistics`. |
| `include/engine/planet/params.hpp` | `PlanetGenerationParams`: every generation knob with documented defaults, plus the derived values `validate()` computes (root cube, octree depth, shell band, sea radius). |
| `include/engine/planet/noise.hpp/.cpp` | OpenSimplex2 2D/3D port (CC0 reference by KdotJPG). Stateless: lattice vertices are hashed through 64-bit primes mixed with the seed, so every call is thread-safe and bit-reproducible. |
| `include/engine/planet/climate.hpp/.cpp` | `ClimateSampler`: latitude/altitude temperature with a perturbation field, moisture from noise plus latitude belts (ITCZ, subtropical highs), Whittaker-style biome classification, and CONTINUOUS biome color blending - albedo interpolates between palette rows over configurable blend widths (temperature/moisture, slope, elevation bands) instead of hard-switching, because per-vertex hard switches smear into pale stains across triangles. Per-point luminance jitter breaks up large biome sheets. |
| `include/engine/planet/density.hpp/.cpp` | `DensityField`: the signed density (meters). Radial relief (domain-warped continents, ridged mountains masked by the continent field, hills, terraces with a cliff mask) plus true 3D terms: overhang displacement, strata banding, optional cave worms. A submarine fade keeps ocean basins smooth; a coastal-flatten band damps the detail fields toward the waterline so shorelines slope into the sea instead of breaking into sub-sea pond webs. `surfaceRadius` bisects the first crossing along a direction; `reliefAt` exposes height plus the mountain/cliff masks (the capability's `sampleRelief`). |
| `include/engine/planet/meshing.hpp/.cpp` | Marching cubes per chunk over a `(n+3)^3` padded grid. All sample positions derive from ONE global lattice index per axis (`-half + step * globalIndex`, steps related by exact powers of two), so any two chunks compute bit-identical positions for shared lattice points: same-depth neighbors weld watertight, cross-depth boundaries stay aligned. Vertex normals average the gradients of the (up to) four cells adjacent to the edge in canonical min-corner form - still a pure function of the edge's global position, but continuous across cell borders (a single-cell gradient is constant per cell and speckles steep creases with dark facets). Biome colors come from the climate sampler's blended palette. Cross-depth boundaries are sealed by CONFORMING BOUNDARY SAMPLING plus the EXTENSION STRIP (see "Seam-free chunk boundaries" below): on face segments whose far cell is a rendered leaf one level coarser, the boundary-plane lattice points sample the coarse lattice's trilinear interpolant instead of the raw field, and the chunk also marches the apron cells just beyond those faces so its surface carries the true isoline into the coarse neighbor's territory, where the two surfaces interpenetrate. Edge crossings clamp the interpolation denominator (identical endpoint densities must not produce inf/NaN vertices - a degenerate triangle spans the whole screen in a software rasterizer). The water pass marches the sea-sphere field with depth-tinted colors and radially auto-oriented triangles; its depth tint samples `densityAt` directly (one pure field, continuous across LOD), and fully buried water cells are skipped so the sea sphere cannot poke grazing-angle slivers through the terrain. |
| `include/engine/planet/octree.hpp/.cpp` | LOD selection: shell-band pruning, corner-probe pruning of fully solid/air subtrees (a bounded-field argument bounds the probe margin; nodes straddling the sea surface are exempt so the ocean cannot get holes), horizon culling (a node is culled when all 8 cube corners sit in the shadow cone of the guaranteed-solid inner shell sphere - the occluded set is a convex cone, so the corner test is exact), and screen-space distance refinement (a node refines while its voxel subtends more than `lod_angular_voxel_radians` at the eye, so detail follows apparent size at any planet radius). A 2:1 balancing fixpoint forces splits along coarse neighbor chains until adjacent rendered leaves differ by at most one depth level; the balancing walks only records refreshed by the CURRENT selection (stale records from earlier eye positions are skipped and pruned, so live balancing cannot silently miss violations). `computeConformMask` derives, per rendered leaf, which face segments border a coarser rendered leaf - the segments are decided from GLOBAL indices (perpendicular + tangential coarse-cell coordinates, diagonal ring included) and the decision ORs the cells on BOTH sides of the plane, so every chunk touching the same boundary point computes the same mask bits. Visible leaves are returned sorted by (depth, x, y, z). |
| `include/engine/planet/planet.hpp/.cpp` | `Planet` facade: owns a validated params copy, the density field and the octree; issues chunk builds to the kernel `TaskScheduler` (`TaskGroup`-tracked, bounded by `max_concurrent_builds` / `max_new_builds_per_update`), collects finished meshes into a stable cache, evicts stale entries (`chunk_eviction_updates`, `chunk_cache_capacity`) and hands out borrowed views. Each chunk build carries its `ConformMask`; the mask set is recomputed whenever the visible list changes (a mask is a pure function of the sorted visible list and the chunk coordinate) and chunks whose stored mesh was built under a different mask are rebuilt - the stale mesh keeps rendering until the conforming rebuild lands. |
| `include/engine/planet/capabilities.hpp` | Public capability types and function-pointer table. |
| `source/planet_module.cpp` | Module singleton: `[planet]` configuration mapping, per-planet creation with description overrides, capability binding, entry table. |
| `demo_planet/` (top level) | The generation demo application: globe/coast/cliffs/surface views with deterministic camera placement, screenshot export, `--self-test`. |

## How it works

- **Determinism.** A chunk mesh is `f(params, coord, conformMask)`: sampling positions, gradient cells, climate colors and MC case tables are all pure functions of the planet parameters, the chunk's integer coordinates and the conforming mask (itself a pure function of the sorted visible list and the coordinate). No shared mutable state participates in a build, so builds can run on any thread in any order; the self-test (`demo_planet --self-test`) builds the same chunk set from three planets in ascending, reversed and rotated orders and compares hash multisets, plus seed-sensitivity and mask-variant (unconstrained / all-conforming / mixed) stability checks. Cross-run determinism follows from integer-exact lattice addressing (no accumulated floats).
- **Overhangs.** The radial relief alone would be a wrapped heightmap. The overhang term adds `simplex3(point / overhang_wavelength) * overhang_amplitude * mask` to the signed density, displacing the iso-surface sideways as well as vertically; strata and (optional) caves are further 3D terms. Together they produce ledges, cave mouths and overhanging cliff faces that a heightmap cannot represent.
- **Climate and biomes.** Temperature = equator-to-pole gradient (configurable `equator_temperature` / `pole_temperature`) minus an altitude lapse rate, perturbed by a noise field. Moisture = base humidity noise plus latitude belts. The (temperature, moisture) pair classifies through a Whittaker-style table into the `Biome` enum; water depth bands, beaches, bare-rock slopes (slope > `rock_slope_threshold`) and an altitude snow line override the table. The rendered color, however, is a CONTINUOUS blend of the palette rows: classification thresholds pick the biome id for consumers, while the albedo interpolates across the same thresholds over the configured blend widths - smooth coasts, snow lines and rock edges without per-vertex stains.
- **Streaming and LOD.** Each frame `updatePlanet` re-selects the visible node set for the eye, requests missing meshes nearest-first and drains finished builds. Refinement stops once a node's voxel subtends at most `lod_angular_voxel_radians` at the eye, so detail concentrates where it is visible; shell-band and corner-probe pruning drop the solid interior and the empty sky; horizon culling removes the planet's far side (exact shadow-cone test against the guaranteed-solid inner sphere, safe for eyes at or near the surface).
- **Seam-free chunk boundaries.** Three mechanisms replace the earlier skirt walls (which read as painted dark stripes between chunks):
  - *Shared lattice.* Same-depth neighbors evaluate bit-identical positions and gradients for every boundary vertex, so their meshes weld exactly - nothing to hide.
  - *2:1 octree balance + fresh-record balancing.* Without balance, the distance rule can place rendered leaves 2+ depth levels apart across a shared face; the coarse chord then departs from the fine isoline over several voxels and LOD rings show cracks. After each selection, a fixpoint walks the 26 face/edge/corner neighbor directions of every rendered leaf, finds the shallowest covering leaf on each neighbor chain, and force-splits the coarse chain until adjacent leaves differ by at most one level. The walk only trusts records refreshed by the CURRENT selection: records left over from earlier eye positions describe a tree that no longer exists, and trusting them silently skips violations (order-dependent crack dashes at the rings). Balancing changes WHICH chunks render, never their geometry.
  - *Conforming boundary sampling + extension strips (cross-depth transitions).* Wherever a face segment borders a rendered leaf one level coarser (the per-segment `ConformMask`), the fine chunk samples that segment's boundary-plane lattice points from the COARSE LATTICE's trilinear interpolant instead of the raw field - at coarse lattice points this degenerates to the coarse sample itself, so the fine isoline and the coarse chords share their exact zero crossings on every coarse grid line. The residual between crossings (the coarse chord sagitta) is covered by the EXTENSION: the fine chunk also marches the apron cells just beyond conforming faces, carrying the true surface INTO the coarse neighbor's territory, where the neighbor's own surface overlaps it. The two surfaces interpenetrate along the whole transition, so no viewing ray can slip between them - the sub-voxel mismatch reads as terrain-colored overlap, never as a crack against the background. The conform decision is SYMMETRIC: it ORs the two depth-(d-1) cells adjacent to the plane at the point's diagonal position (one seen from each side), because the two sides would otherwise consult different cells and disagree at chunk corners. Meshes remain pure functions of `(params, coord, conformMask)`.

## Threading and lifetime

`updatePlanet`, `copyVisibleChunks` and the sampling calls are main-thread (capability calls are main-thread only). Chunk builds run on kernel scheduler workers against the pure build path; results flow through a mutex-guarded handoff list into the main-thread cache. `Planet` owns a copy of its params (the capability receives descriptions as stack temporaries), and `~Planet` drains in-flight builds before the field dies. Borrowed chunk views stay valid until the next `updatePlanet` call.

## Configuration

See `configuration.md` for every `[planet]` key with ranges and effects. All lengths are meters; planets scale to arbitrary radii by raising `radius_meters` together with the wavelengths/amplitudes that should scale with it.
