Surface textures for the planet demo (modules/planet/docs/configuration.md documents how the generator tiles them). All of them come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), published under the CC0 1.0 Universal license (https://polyhaven.com/license); they are unmodified scans apart from the processing noted below, and none is generated by this project. Each surface group carries a SET of scanned variants (see the chunk renderer's setTerrainTextures): every chunk's draw picks one variant deterministically from the chunk address, so neighboring chunks photograph different scans and no single scan repeats across the visible disk, and the generator's own anti-repetition stages (the texture_uv_warp_* keys and texture_chunk_rotation) break the remaining tile-scale repetition. The albedo maps are prepared for the renderer's linear-space multiply: the sRGB-encoded scan is desaturated to 45% saturation toward its stored-space luminance (the geologic-province and biome vertex colors carry the hue through the multiply, while the scans keep their luminance structure), decoded to linear values, then scaled so the mean luminance is exactly 1.0, clipped back to the 0..1 byte range, and stored as JPEG at quality 92. At load time the renderer computes each texture's mean over its decoded pixels (RenderCapability::textureMean), and the chunk draw divides the material base color by the selected variant's mean, so every variant's multiply preserves the vertex colors' overall brightness. The normal maps are the assets' OpenGL-convention tangent-space maps resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS).

    Source asset "aerial ground rock" (https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_ground_rock), 1k diffuse map, processed with the pipeline above. Second ground-group variant: a dry soil-and-rock mosaic whose warmer, browner tone alternates with ground grey's neutral soil between chunks.

    Source asset "dry ground rocks" (https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_rocks), 1k diffuse map, processed with the pipeline above. Third ground-group variant: gravelly dry earth with embedded stones for the arid end of the ground variation.
cliff_side_diff_1k.jpg and cliff_side_nor_gl_1k.jpg
    Source asset "cliff side" (https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side), shipped with the original demo and retained as the first rock-group variant: the 2k diffuse map resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS), desaturated to 45% saturation, linearized and mean-normalized, plus its 2k OpenGL-convention normal map resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS).

    Source asset "rocky terrain 02" (https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02), 2k diffuse and OpenGL-convention normal maps resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS), the diffuse map processed with the pipeline above. Second rock-group variant: mossy broken rock country whose relief structure differs from cliff side's sedimentary beds.

    Source asset "rock face" (https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face), 2k diffuse and OpenGL-convention normal maps resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS), the diffuse map processed with the pipeline above. Third rock-group variant: a massive, smoother rock wall that keeps large-scale luminance variation instead of fine bedding.
