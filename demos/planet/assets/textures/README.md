Surface textures for the planet demo (modules/planet/docs/configuration.md documents how the generator tiles them). The rock and soil scans come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), published under the CC0 1.0 Universal license (https://polyhaven.com/license); they are unmodified scans apart from the processing noted below. The grass detail pair is composed procedurally by the repository's own script (scripts/compose_terrain_detail.py), which regenerates it byte-identically from a fixed seed. Each surface group carries a SET of scanned variants (see the chunk renderer's setTerrainTextures): every chunk's draw picks one variant deterministically from the chunk address, so neighboring chunks photograph different scans and no single scan repeats across the visible disk, and the generator's own anti-repetition stages (the texture_uv_warp_* keys and texture_chunk_rotation) break the remaining tile-scale repetition. The albedo maps are prepared for the renderer's linear-space multiply: the sRGB-encoded scan is desaturated to 45% saturation toward its stored-space luminance (the geologic-province and biome vertex colors carry the hue through the multiply, while the scans keep their luminance structure), decoded to linear values, then scaled so the mean luminance is exactly 1.0, clipped back to the 0..1 byte range, and stored as JPEG at quality 92. At load time the renderer computes each texture's mean over its decoded pixels (RenderCapability::textureMean), and the chunk draw divides the material base color by the selected variant's mean, so every variant's multiply preserves the vertex colors' overall brightness. The normal maps are the assets' OpenGL-convention tangent-space maps resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS).

grass_detail_diff_1k.png and grass_detail_nor_gl_1k.png
    Composed by scripts/compose_terrain_detail.py (deterministic, fixed seed): a 1024x1024 tileable grass-ground detail pair. The diffuse map is a near-neutral multiply - sparse blade streaks with wind-jetted leans and tip-bright shading over a mottled soil base with dryness patches and pebble speckles - so the multiply carries the vertex albedo's hue while the near-ground surface reads as a sward of individual blades over soil instead of smooth vertex color. The blade tone band stays NARROW around the soil's own luminance and the mottle runs three wrapped noise octaves (a broad patch scale, a mid soil scale and a fine grain): a tile whose stamps sit far from its base photographs every repeat as the same dark-on-light motif once minified - the eye matches the tile lattice instead of the grass - so the composition keeps every stamp within about ten percent of the base tone and lets the mip chain melt the stamps into the sward's grain at mid range, while the soil-patch octave keeps large-scale variation alive under heavy minification. The normal map encodes the relief of the SAME height field (the blade streaks ride above the soil), in the renderer's own tangent-frame convention (x along increasing u, y along increasing v, z out of the surface). The renderer's per-variant mean compensation renormalizes the multiply, exactly as with the scanned variants. First ground-group variant.

dirt_nor_gl_1k.jpg
    Source asset "dirt" (https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt), 1k OpenGL-convention normal map, unmodified apart from the pipeline's resize stage (the 1k asset is already 1024x1024). Ground-group relief map paired with ground grey's albedo: fine soil-crumb and clod structure, isotropic in every direction, so the near-ground sward shades with per-sample relief under the sun instead of the smooth vertex gradient (the relief gate keeps its sampling to the eye's feet; see the chunk renderer's ground emit).
