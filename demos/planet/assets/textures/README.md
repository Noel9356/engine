Surface textures for the planet demo (modules/planet/docs/configuration.md
documents how the generator tiles them). All three come from Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com), published under the CC0 1.0 Universal license
(https://polyhaven.com/license); they are unmodified scans apart from the
processing noted below, and none is generated by this project. The two
albedo maps are prepared for the renderer's linear-space multiply: the
sRGB-encoded scan is decoded to linear values, then scaled so the mean
luminance is exactly 1.0 (with a small contrast gain around that mean), so
the multiply preserves the vertex colors' overall brightness.

ground_grey_diff_1k.jpg
    Source asset "ground grey" (https://polyhaven.com/a/ground_grey),
    1k diffuse map, used as the ground surface group's detail texture.
cliff_side_diff_1k.jpg
    Source asset "cliff side" (https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side),
    2k diffuse map resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS), desaturated to 45%
    saturation (the rock's strata banding keeps its luminance structure
    while the geologic-province vertex colors carry the hue through the
    multiply), linearized and mean-normalized. Used as the rock surface
    group's detail texture.
cliff_side_nor_gl_1k.jpg
    Source asset "cliff side" (https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side),
    2k OpenGL-convention normal map resized to 1024x1024 (LANCZOS). Used as
    the rock surface group's normal map.
