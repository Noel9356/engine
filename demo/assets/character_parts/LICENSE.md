# character_parts licensing

The meshes in this directory come from freely licensed sources; every
downloaded pack is released under CC0 1.0 (Universal Public Domain
Dedication) by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com):

- `torso_humanoid.obj` - torso/joint partition (root..neck + clavicles) of the
  Superhero male in the *Universal Base Characters* pack.
- `arm_left.obj`, `arm_right.obj` - the upperarm/hand chains of the same
  character (one source side per engine side, no mirroring), rooted with a
  fused ball joint.
- `leg_left.obj`, `leg_right.obj` - the thigh/foot chains of the same
  character (buried interface skirt at the hip).
- `head_demon.obj` - the Demon's `Head`-joint surface in *Ultimate Monsters*
  (head mesh only, minus the floating halo accessory, anchored at the measured
  skull/neck boundary; colors are sampled from the pack's monster atlas with a
  per-face vote). One Loop subdivision level; the collar under the skull is
  generated by the pipeline.
- `head_dragon.obj` - the Dragon Evolved `Head`-joint surface in
  *Ultimate Monsters*.
- `head_serpent.obj` - the Dino's `Head`-joint surface in *Ultimate
  Monsters*.
- `wing_left.obj`, `wing_right.obj` - the Dragon's wing chains in
  *Ultimate Monsters* (each engine side keeps its own source chain), rooted
  with a ball joint; open-boundary membrane triangles are double-sided.
- `tail_reptile.obj`, `tail_reptile_tip.obj` - procedural reptile tail
  (base + tip chain) authored by the pipeline itself. The earlier revision
  cut the tail from the *Dinosaur Animated Pack* T-Rex, but that pack is no
  longer retrievable upstream (download quota), and the audit found the
  shipped cut held wing geometry instead of a tail; the tail is now generated
  along the `tail_chain` rig curve like the procedural neck transition piece.

The extraction pipeline lives in `scripts/build_character_parts.py` at the
engine project root. It (re)generates the demon head and the tail pieces from
the sources in `assets_dl/Ultimate Monsters/` (an earlier revision of the
script covered the remaining parts; those checked-in OBJs are unchanged):
skinned sources are cut along their rig partition (a part is exactly what its
joint moves, so the cut follows the animation boundary), texture/material
colors are sampled into per-vertex colors (tiny atlases are gutter-inpainted
and sampled with a per-face seven-point vote), chunky cuts receive one Loop
subdivision level, shading normals are crease-aware per corner, and every
junction away from a part's origin gets either a canonical interface ring
with a color-matched collar (heads, legs) or a fused ball joint (arms, wings,
tail). Parts whose interface ring is not at their origin also ship baked
morphs (`m`/`d` blocks, indexed after the vertex weld) whose interface deltas
match the runtime anchor bindings; the shared constants are emitted as
`demo/source/demo/character_metrics.gen.hpp`. The OBJs here are the
checked-in outputs so the demo runs without network access; re-running the
script refreshes the regenerated parts from the downloaded pack. Only the
neck transition piece is procedural in the demo itself (see
`demo/source/demo/character_content.cpp`).
